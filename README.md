# Guest

*The world that lives without you.*

Guest is a pack of NeoForge mods for Minecraft 26.1. It makes the world keep going on its own: weather moves across regions, villages build and trade, caves fill up with dens, and the ancient cities turn out to have someone living in them.

Where possible everything is built on top of vanilla mechanics. The mods adds no new items, only a few blocks and one mob.

All 5 addons require Core mod to install, each addon can work without other addons, but not without Core. 

## Atmosphere

- Regional weather. It can rain over one village and be clear a kilometer away. Storms, blizzards, sandstorms, fog, heat, leaf fall, and on clear winter nights, the aurora.
- Heavy weather actually gets in the way: in a blizzard you can barely see, and the way home is by the light of windows and the sound of village bells.
- The land keeps traces of the weather. Snow drifts against doors, rain turns paths to mud, ice forms at the shore, icicles grow under roofs, moss creeps over old stone. Lightning burns a tree down to charcoal.
- Plants can now be covered in snow, snow layers can lay in several layers and leaves now turn white when it's cold, just like in bedrock edition.

## Wilds

- Caves have dens: zombies, skeletons, spiders and creepers live in them, come out at dusk and go back before dawn.
- Animals gather into herds that graze, migrate and hide from storms.
- Places where people walk turn into paths. Roads appear between villages and grow over when nobody uses them.
- Cut trees grow back.

## Settlements

- Villagers actually do their jobs, and villages build and expand themselves even when you're not around.
- They react to what happens: storms, the moon, the aurora. Sometimes they have rituals.
- Cartographers go on expeditions, people emigrate along the roads, caravans travel between villages, and sometimes they don't arrive.
- Illagers live as a society too: patrols, camps, rituals. A lone pillager might even settle down in a village.
- Villagers remember what you did. The stories about you get passed around and retold, and change on the way.

## Architects

Some ancient cities are still inhabited. Find them and figure out what they are hiding.

## Hands

- Crafting tables, furnaces, anvils, enchanting tables and brewing stands are physical: you put items on them and take them off, instead of dropping everything into a menu.
- A pickaxe lets you climb walls with right click.
- Sleeping is slow, and the world keeps going while you do it.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2
- NeoForge, or Fabric with Fabric API and Forge Config API Port

## Installation

Put the jars for your loader (`-neoforge` or `-fabric`) into your `mods` folder: Guest Core and any of the addons.
